MISSION 02

NexusPON NOC

FTTH · GPON / EPON · TELEGRAM CHATOPS

Technicians used to log in to each OLT by hand and cross-check the RADIUS billing panel and the ACS in separate tabs just to tell a fiber cut from a power outage. NexusPON polls nine OLTs (ZTE over Telnet, HSGQ through read-only adapters), caches optical readings and lines them up with live PPPoE sessions. A companion Telegram bot lets technicians check a customer or list pending ONUs with a single command.

Dulu teknisi harus login ke tiap OLT satu per satu lalu mencocokkan panel billing RADIUS dan ACS di tab terpisah hanya untuk membedakan kabel putus dengan listrik padam. NexusPON membaca sembilan OLT (ZTE lewat Telnet, HSGQ lewat adapter read-only), menyimpan cache data optik, dan menyandingkannya dengan sesi PPPoE yang sedang aktif. Bot Telegram pendamping memungkinkan teknisi mengecek pelanggan atau melihat ONU pending dengan satu perintah.

MISSION 03

SekolahRCM

SCHOOL PORTAL · ONLINE ADMISSIONS (SPMB)

The official website and admissions system for SDIT Ruby Cendekia Madani, an Islamic primary school in Sukabumi. Staff edit content in a headless CMS and parents register and upload documents online. Uploads are checked by file signature, writes are atomic so a power cut cannot corrupt data, and every application syncs to the committee's Google Sheet and Drive. Admin access uses Google sign-in with role checks. It runs in Docker on a self-hosted server behind Cloudflare.

Website resmi dan sistem pendaftaran SDIT Ruby Cendekia Madani, sekolah dasar Islam terpadu di Sukabumi. Staf mengubah konten lewat headless CMS dan orang tua mendaftar serta mengunggah dokumen secara online. Unggahan dicek berdasarkan signature file, penulisan data bersifat atomik agar tidak rusak saat listrik padam, dan setiap pendaftaran tersinkron ke Google Sheet dan Drive panitia. Akses admin memakai login Google dengan pengecekan peran. Semuanya berjalan di Docker pada server mandiri di belakang Cloudflare.
